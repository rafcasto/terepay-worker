# Client Behavior Assessment Prompts for Credit Risk

Systematic Review Questions for Bank Statement Analysis

1\. BNPL (Buy-Now-Pay-Later) Dependency Assessment

Count and Calculate:

* "How many Afterpay/ZIP/Laybuy transactions in the last 60 days?"  
* "What is the total monthly BNPL spending vs. net income percentage?"  
* "Are there daily BNPL transactions (red flag if \>3 per week)?"

Pattern Analysis:

* "Are BNPL services used for necessities (groceries, fuel, bills) or discretionary items?"  
* "Is there evidence of BNPL cycling \- using one service to pay another?"  
* "Are transaction amounts getting smaller over time (desperation indicator)?"

Critical Thresholds:

* BNPL \>$300/month \= High Risk  
* BNPL \>30% of income \= Critical Risk  
* Daily BNPL usage \= Immediate Decline  
  2\. Cash Flow Stress Indicators

Balance Pattern Questions:

* "What is the lowest account balance in each of the last 3 months?"  
* "How many days per month does the balance drop below $100?"  
* "Does the balance hit near-zero within 3 days of payday?"

Emergency Behavior:

* "Are there ATM withdrawals when balance is under $50?"  
* "Multiple small transactions when account is critically low?"  
* "Evidence of 'micro-spending' ($1-5 purchases) during low balance periods?"

Critical Indicators:

* Balance under $50 for \>15 days/month \= High Risk  
* Balance under $20 for \>7 days/month \= Critical Risk  
* Zero balance more than once per month \= Immediate Decline  
  3\. Credit Dependency Patterns

Active Credit Assessment:

* "How many different credit sources are being used simultaneously?"  
* "Are credit payments being made with other credit facilities?"  
* "Evidence of cash advances or loan top-ups?"  
  Family Financial Stress:\*\*  
* "Are there frequent emergency transfers from family/friends?"  
* "Pattern of borrowing from personal contacts?"  
* "Irregular large deposits suggesting asset sales or emergency help?"

Remittance Stress Indicators:

* "Are overseas transfers sent even when client's account is critically low?"  
* "Evidence of choosing remittances over essential local expenses?"  
* "Pattern of financial stress immediately after large overseas transfers?"

Warning Signs:

* 3 active credit facilities \= High Risk  
* Using credit to pay credit \= Critical Risk  
* Weekly family bailouts \= Immediate Decline  
* Remittances sent while account under $100 \= High Risk  
* Undisclosed family obligations abroad \= Moderate Risk  
  4\. Gambling and Addiction Screening

Direct Questions:

* "Any transactions to gambling platforms, betting apps, or casinos?"  
* "Pattern of large withdrawals followed by immediate re-deposits?"  
* "Late-night transaction patterns at entertainment venues?"

Substance-Related Spending:

* "Frequency of liquor store purchases (daily/multiple weekly \= concern)?"  
* "High-frequency tobacco shop visits?"  
* "Unexplained cash withdrawals in entertainment districts?"

Zero Tolerance Indicators:

* Any gambling activity \= Immediate Decline  
* Daily alcohol purchases \= Critical Risk  
* Substance-related spending \>10% income \= High Risk  
  5\. Financial Planning Capability

Savings and Buffer Assessment:

* "Has the client maintained a $200+ buffer at any point in 3 months?"  
* "Evidence of planned purchases vs. impulse buying?"  
* "Bill payment patterns \- scheduled or reactive?"

Budgeting Evidence:

* "Are expenses consistent week-to-week or chaotic?"  
* "Evidence of comparison shopping or just immediate purchasing?"  
* "Pattern of financial preparation for known expenses?"

Planning Indicators:

* No savings buffer ever \= High Risk  
* Chaotic spending patterns \= Moderate Risk  
* Reactive bill payments \= Moderate Risk  
  6\. Remittance and Family Support Assessment

Overseas Transfer Patterns:

* "How many international money transfers in the last 3 months?"  
* "What percentage of income is sent overseas regularly?"  
* "Are remittances sent during personal financial stress periods?"

Family Obligation Analysis:

* "Are overseas transfers consistent or crisis-driven?"  
* "Evidence of supporting multiple family members abroad?"  
* "Remittance amounts increasing over time despite stable income?"

Critical Assessment Questions:

* "Are disclosed dependents accurate or are there hidden family obligations?"  
* "Is client prioritizing overseas support over local financial stability?"  
* "Evidence of pressure from family for financial support?"

Remittance Risk Indicators:

* Overseas transfers \>25% of income \= High Risk  
* Sending money while using BNPL for basics \= Critical Risk  
* Undisclosed remittance obligations \= High Risk  
* Crisis-driven large transfers \= Moderate Risk  
  7\. Lifestyle vs. Income Alignment

Spending Proportion Analysis:

* "What percentage of income goes to dining out/takeaways?"  
* "Entertainment spending relative to income level?"  
* "Transport costs \- necessary vs. convenience spending?"

Luxury vs. Necessity:

* "Premium brand purchases despite financial stress?"  
* "Multiple subscription services while struggling with basics?"  
* "High-end purchases followed by BNPL for essentials?"

Misalignment Flags:

* Dining out \>20% of income \= Moderate Risk  
* Luxury purchases while using BNPL for basics \= High Risk  
* Entertainment \>15% of income \= Moderate Risk  
  Risk Scoring Matrix  
  Critical Risk Score (Automatic Decline)

Answer YES to 3+ of these:

* BNPL usage \>30% of income?  
* Balance under $50 for \>50% of month?  
* Any gambling activity present?  
* Using credit to pay other credit?  
* Daily BNPL transactions?  
  High Risk Score (Senior Management Required)

Answer YES to 2 Critical OR 4+ of these:

* BNPL usage \>$300/month?  
* Balance under $100 for \>15 days/month?  
* 3 active credit facilities?  
* Substance-related spending patterns?  
* No savings buffer maintained?  
* Weekly family financial bailouts?  
* Overseas remittances \>25% of income?  
* Sending money abroad while using BNPL for essentials?  
  Moderate Risk Score (Enhanced Documentation)

Answer YES to 1 Critical OR 2-3 High Risk:

* BNPL usage \>$200/month?  
* Balance under $100 for \>7 days/month?  
* Chaotic spending patterns?  
* Bill payment irregularities?  
* Lifestyle spending \>25% of income?  
  Implementation Checklist  
  For Each Application:  
1. Count specific transactions (don't estimate)  
2. Calculate exact percentages (income vs. spending categories)  
3. Document patterns with dates (establish trends)  
4. Note cumulative risk factors (multiple minor flags \= major concern)  
5. Record specific observations (audit trail requirements)  
   Decision Documentation:  
* "Based on \[specific findings\], this application presents \[risk level\]"  
* "Key concerns: \[list numbered observations\]"  
* "Affordability impact: \[calculate specific hardship risk\]"  
* "CCCFA compliance: \[explain how decision prevents substantial hardship\]"  
  Quality Control Questions:  
* "Would this loan payment cause substantial hardship based on the evidence?"  
* "Are we making assumptions or using verified data?"  
* "Have we considered all reasonable explanations for concerning patterns?"  
* "Does our decision protect the applicant's long-term financial wellbeing?"  
  Staff Training Reminders  
* Use objective criteria, not subjective judgments  
* Focus on financial capacity, not lifestyle choices  
* Document everything with specific examples  
* Consider cultural contexts that might explain patterns  
* Aim to prevent hardship, not judge character  
* Remember: declining a harmful loan protects the customer
