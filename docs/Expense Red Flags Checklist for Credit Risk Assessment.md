# Expense Red Flags Checklist for Credit Risk Assessment

# **Critical Red Flags \- Immediate Decline Considerations**

1\. Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) Abuse Patterns

* Multiple daily BNPL transactions (Afterpay, ZIP, etc.)  
* BNPL usage exceeding $300+ monthly  
* Overlapping BNPL services (using 3+ providers simultaneously)  
* Small frequent BNPL purchases ($5-20) indicating desperation  
* BNPL for basic necessities (food, fuel, utilities)

2\. Cash Flow Distress Indicators

* Account balance frequently under $50  
* Multiple overdraft fees or dishonor charges  
* Paycheck-to-paycheck living (balance drops to near zero before next pay)  
* Emergency cash withdrawals from ATMs during low balance periods  
* Micro-transactions when account balance is critically low

3\. Credit Dependency Patterns

* Daily credit card or loan advances  
* Using credit to pay other credit obligations  
* Multiple payday loan providers  
* Borrowing from family/friends (frequent personal transfers in)  
* Selling items (irregular deposit patterns suggesting asset liquidation)

# High Risk Indicators

4\. Gambling-Related Expenses

* Online gambling platforms (any amount)  
* Sports betting apps  
* Casino transactions  
* Lottery/scratchcard patterns  
* Irregular large withdrawals followed by immediate deposits

5\. Substance/Addiction-Related Spending

* Frequent alcohol store purchases (daily/multiple weekly)  
* High-frequency tobacco shop visits  
* Unexplained cash withdrawals in addiction-prone areas  
* Late-night transaction patterns at entertainment venues

6\. Impulse Shopping Behaviors

* Multiple same-day retail purchases  
* High-frequency online shopping (daily Amazon, clothing sites)  
* Purchase-return cycles (buying then returning frequently)  
* Brand-heavy spending despite income level (luxury brands on low income)

# Moderate Risk Flags

7\. Financial Management Issues

* No emergency savings buffer (never maintains $200+ balance)  
* Bill payment irregularity (utilities, insurance paid sporadically)  
* Fee accumulation (multiple bank fees, late payment charges)  
* No budgeting evidence (chaotic spending without patterns)

8\. Family Financial Strain

* Frequent family bailouts (receiving emergency transfers)  
* Supporting multiple dependents beyond disclosed obligations  
* Irregular large family transfers out (suggesting family financial crises)  
* Co-signed obligation failures (payments for others' debts)

9\. Lifestyle Inflation Warnings

* Dining out frequency exceeding 20% of income  
* Entertainment spending above income-appropriate levels  
* Subscription service accumulation (multiple streaming, gym, etc.)  
* Transport costs exceeding practical needs (frequent rideshares, premium fuel)

## Assessment Prompts for Each Category

BNPL Assessment Questions:

* How many BNPL transactions in the last 30 days?  
* What's the total monthly BNPL commitment vs. income?  
* Are BNPL services being used for essentials or luxuries?  
* Is there evidence of BNPL cycling (paying off one with another)?

Cash Flow Assessment:

* What's the average monthly minimum balance?  
* How many days per month is the balance under $100?  
* Are there patterns of financial stress around bill due dates?  
* Is income immediately consumed upon receipt?

Credit Dependency Check:

* How many active credit facilities?  
* Is new credit being used to service existing credit?  
* Are there signs of credit shopping (multiple recent inquiries)?  
* Is the applicant dependent on credit for basic living expenses?

Behavioral Pattern Analysis:

* Are spending patterns consistent with stated income?  
* Is there evidence of financial planning or just reactive spending?  
* Are discretionary expenses reasonable for the income level?  
* Does spending behavior suggest underlying issues?

## Risk Scoring Framework

Critical Risk (Automatic Decline)

* 3+ Critical Red Flags present  
* BNPL monthly usage \>30% of income  
* Account balance under $50 for \>50% of month  
* Evidence of gambling or substance-related spending

High Risk (Enhanced Assessment Required)

* 2 Critical Red Flags OR 4+ High Risk indicators  
* Requires senior management approval  
* Maximum loan amount restrictions  
* Enhanced monitoring required

Moderate Risk (Standard Assessment)

* 1 Critical Red Flag OR 2-3 High Risk indicators  
* Standard underwriting with noted concerns  
* Consider lower loan amounts  
* May require additional documentation

Lower Risk (Standard Processing)

* Minimal red flags present  
* Spending patterns align with income  
* Evidence of financial planning  
* Regular savings behavior visible

Implementation Notes

1. Use this checklist systematically for every application  
2. Document specific observations for audit trail  
3. Consider cumulative effect of multiple minor flags  
4. Update based on emerging spending patterns and economic conditions  
5. Train staff to recognize these patterns without making moral judgments about applicants  
6. Focus on affordability and hardship prevention, not lifestyle choices

## Legal Compliance Reminders

* These flags support responsible lending obligations  
* Document how findings support the affordability assessment  
* Ensure decisions are based on financial capacity, not personal judgment  
* Maintain objective, factual records of spending pattern analysis  
* Consider cultural and circumstantial factors that may explain spending patterns
